Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers — call us for anything else.

How long are the courses?

Most courses run between 3 and 6 months, depending on the track and batch timing. Short-term skill modules are also available. Call us for the current batch schedule and timings.

Who can join? Do I need an IT background?

Students who have passed 10th or 12th, graduates, and working professionals can all join. No prior IT background is needed for beginner tracks — we start from the basics and build up with practical training.

Is the certificate valid for jobs?

Yes. On successful completion you receive a government-recognised certificate. You can include it in job applications and your resume, and it reflects the practical skills covered in your course.

What placement support do you provide?

We provide dedicated placement assistance: resume building, interview preparation, and connecting students with hiring companies. Final selection depends on your performance and the employer's requirements.
